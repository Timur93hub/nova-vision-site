Reference

A model consistency sheet — several angles and framings of one face and pose. It locks the look so it stays identical in every shot of the series.

Generation

Nano Banana Pro and GPT Image 2.5 turn the reference into a finished photo: model, location, light and textures come together in one scene.

Video

Kling 3.0 and Seedance 2.5 add motion — wind, steps, a turn of the head — turning a still into a living scene for Reels and ads.
